# Distilling a Video World Model into a Connectome-Wired Brain
### FlyWire-constrained latent prediction for navigation and object arrangement

## One-sentence claim
A 300M-parameter video world model (V-JEPA 2.1) can be distilled into a recurrent network whose
*only* recurrent wiring is the real synapse-level connectome of ~5k Drosophila neurons (FlyWire v783,
four "brains": visual projection, central complex, mushroom-body output, descending), and the real
wiring is a better inductive bias for action-conditioned latent prediction and teacher-free planning
than any degree-matched control. (An earlier version of this claim included "emergent EPG head-direction
coding"; dropped after #7: the EPG population carries R² ≤ 0.2 of heading, and whole-population heading
decodability must first beat a ridge on the cumulative action counts, which exposes relative heading
linearly when turns are discrete and fixed-angle.)

## Thought experiment ("the fly in the dark")
A fly's central complex keeps a heading estimate and a goal vector for seconds in darkness, using
self-motion only (path integration). A video world model does the same thing in latent space: given
the current latent and an action, predict the latent of the next frame. The thought experiment: if we
force the student's recurrence to *be* the fly's wiring diagram, and ask it to predict a frontier
world model's future latents in darkness, does the wiring that evolution found for this job also
make the distillation easier? If so, the connectome is not just a sparsity pattern; it is a
*task-shaped prior*, and the EPG population inside the student should start to behave like the
biological ring attractor without ever being told about heading.

## What is new (positioning against prior art)
* Prior connectome-constrained models (Lappalainen et al. 2024 Nature; Robinson et al. 2022;
  Shiozaki/Hulse models) fit neural recordings or hand-designed tasks. We instead distil a
  self-supervised video foundation model, turning the connectome into a student world model
  that plans (latent MPC) with no teacher at test time.
* Prior world-model distillation shrinks transformers into smaller transformers/RNNs. We shrink
  into a fixed, signed (Dale's law), biologically-sourced sparse recurrence and show the wiring
  identity, not just its density or degree sequence, matters.
* "Multiple connected brains": we scale capacity by cloning the whole fly brain K times and
  coupling clones through the real cross-hemisphere (commissural) edges only.

## Controls (fixed: neuron count, region labels, I/O pathways, edge count)
connectome (signed)  |  connectome without signs  |  degree-preserving shuffle  |  Erdos-Renyi random
|  dense  |  parameter-matched GRU.

## Experiments
E1 Blind-horizon latent prediction (8 visible steps, then 1..8 blind steps) on NAV and ARRANGE.
E2 Teacher-free planning: student-only latent MPC in the live arena, success rate.
E3 Head-direction decodability: linear probe from EPG / CX / VIS / MB populations, visible and blind
   (path-integration) conditions, reported against (i) a ridge on cumulative one-hot actions alone and (ii) a
   feature-count-matched random 1,400-neuron subset (#7). Optional: collect with `--turn_noise` so turn angles
   are continuous and the action record no longer determines heading.
E4 Multi-brain scaling K = 1,2,3.
E5 Ablations: sign (Dale), region input routing, horizon.
E6 Data efficiency: n_train in {50, 100, 200, 500, 1000} arena episodes, connectome vs degree-shuffled vs GRU, 3 seeds
   (`train.py --n_train`; the experiment that can beat the GRU where a prior should matter).
E7 Real-video transfer: handheld phone walking footage (no labels) -> episodes via `scripts/ingest_video.py` (egomotion
   from patch phase correlation, quantised to the 5 arena actions; continuous yaw / expansion kept as `ego`), evaluated
   zero-shot and with leave-one-clip-out fine-tuning by `scripts/eval_ckpt.py` for every K=1 checkpoint, against the
   trivial predictors on the same episodes. Tags `_realvid`, `_realvid_ft` in summarize.py.

## Honest risk register (what reviewers will attack)
* Synthetic arena, not real video. Mitigation in paper: add an ego-video benchmark (e.g. Habitat / RECON)
  before submission; the pipeline is video-agnostic.
* Single teacher size (ViT-B at 256px for compute). Mitigation: ViT-L run on a GPU.
* Scale of controls: we match edges, not capacity of arbitrary architectures; GRU baseline helps.

## Implementation record (what the code actually does; manuscript text must match this, #12)
* **Loss.** Prediction only: weighted MSE between the student readout and the whitened PCA-256 teacher latent at
  every step (blind steps weighted 3x), plus 1e-4 * mean(h^2). There is no feature-alignment term and no policy
  imitation in any reported run. `scripts/train.py --lambda_act > 0` adds a cross-entropy next-action head on h for
  the ablation; until it is run, the manuscript must describe the loss as prediction only and drop the
  L_feat / L_pred / imitation terms.
* **Action space and episodes.** 5 discrete actions (stay, forward, turn left 22.5 deg, turn right 22.5 deg, back);
  24-step episodes; arena: 360 deg cylindrical panorama at 256 px, fly-eye input 32x8x3; NYC (Valdrada): forward
  110 deg camera at 256 px, forward 4 m / back 2 m, goals 30-60 m away, success < 12 m.
* **Teacher.** V-JEPA 2.1 **ViT-B** (`apiantonio/vjepa2.1-vit-base-384`), 16-frame clips (each env step duplicated
  into one tubelet), per-step target = mean token concatenated with 2x2 pooled tokens = 3,840-d, projected to
  PCA-256 fit on the training set (94.6 % variance on the arena, 91.0 % on NYC) and whitened by a single scalar.
* **Connectome.** FlyWire v783 (`proofread_connections_783`, `neuron_annotations`), 6,935 neurons: VIS 2,330
  (TuBu, MeTu, LC10, LPLC), CX 2,875 (cell_class CX), MB 427 (MBON + DAN), OUT 1,303 (descending); 91,663 directed
  edges with >= 5 synapses (summed over synapse sites); sign from the presynaptic neuron's predicted neurotransmitter
  (ACh/DA/5-HT/OA +, Glu/GABA -), 49.9 % excitatory. Licence CC-BY-NC 4.0 (FlyWire), so the derived
  `data/connectome.npz` is not redistributed here.
* **Student.** h <- (1-a) h + a tanh(gain * (W h + W_in o * visible + W_act a + b)), a = 0.5; W = sign * softplus(w)
  on the edge list (Dale's law) except for the unsigned and dense variants; visual input enters VIS neurons only, the
  one-hot action enters CX and OUT neurons; linear readout from all neurons. Parameters: 7.25 M for K=1, of which
  91.6 k are synapses (the rest is W_in 768x6,935 and W_out 6,935x256). Evaluated on the edge list (O(E) per step)
  since #11; K copies are coupled only through the real cross-hemisphere edges, brain i -> brain i+1.
* **Multi-brain.** K=2 gives no gain (0.621 vs 0.629); K=3 had not completed before #11. A "fixed-total-parameter
  single-brain" control is not well defined for this architecture because >98 % of the parameters sit in the I/O
  projections, which are identical for every K; the honest comparison is K=1 vs K=3 at equal I/O.
* **Statistics.** Run 1: 2 seeds (connectome, shuffled, random), 1 seed elsewhere, no CIs. Required before any
  claim: `scripts/run_seeds.sh` (5 seeds arena, 3 NYC, bootstrap CIs and paired tests from `summarize.py`, #8).

## Connectome subgraph: rationale and ablations (#13)
The student is a CX-centric navigation circuit, not a whole-brain model: VIS supplies the visual projection
neurons that actually feed the central complex (TuBu/MeTu ring inputs) plus the optic-glomeruli LC/LPLC types; CX
is the compass / path-integration / steering machinery (EPG, PEN, Delta7, PFN, PFL, hDelta, FC, ER, ExR); MB
output and dopaminergic neurons are the valence / memory interface; descending neurons are the motor output. Visual
input is injected at the VIS projection neurons rather than through the optic lobe because the optic lobe (~70 k
neurons) would dominate the neuron count while contributing nothing the teacher's latent does not already encode,
and because its proofreading state in v783 is less complete. The 5-synapse threshold is the usual FlyWire default
for "strong" connections. All three choices are ablated by `scripts/ablate_connectome.sh`: min_syn in {1, 5, 10};
regions VIS+CX+OUT (no MB), VIS+CX+MB (no OUT), VIS+CX only; each with the connectome and degree-shuffled wirings so
the wiring-identity gap is measured under every subgraph (`python -m wm2cx.connectome --min_syn --regions --out`).
