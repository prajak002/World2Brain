# Distilling a Video World Model into a Connectome-Wired Brain
### FlyWire-constrained latent prediction for navigation and object arrangement

## One-sentence claim
A 300M-parameter video world model (V-JEPA 2.1) can be distilled into a recurrent network whose
*only* recurrent wiring is the real synapse-level connectome of ~5k Drosophila neurons (FlyWire v783,
four "brains": visual projection, central complex, mushroom-body output, descending), and the real
wiring is a better inductive bias for action-conditioned latent prediction, teacher-free planning,
and emergent head-direction coding than any degree-matched control.

## Thought experiment ("the fly in the dark")
A fly's central complex keeps a heading estimate and a goal vector for seconds in darkness, using
self-motion only (path integration). A video world model does the same thing in latent space: given
the current latent and an action, predict the latent of the next frame. The thought experiment: if we
force the student's recurrence to *be* the fly's wiring diagram, and ask it to predict a frontier
world model's future latents in darkness, does the wiring that evolution found for this job also
make the distillation easier? If so, the connectome is not just a sparsity pattern; it is a
*task-shaped prior*, and the EPG population inside the student should start to behave like the
biological ring attractor without ever being told about heading.

## What is new (positioning against prior art)
* Prior connectome-constrained models (Lappalainen et al. 2024 Nature; Robinson et al. 2022;
  Shiozaki/Hulse models) fit neural recordings or hand-designed tasks. We instead distil a
  self-supervised video foundation model, turning the connectome into a student world model
  that plans (latent MPC) with no teacher at test time.
* Prior world-model distillation shrinks transformers into smaller transformers/RNNs. We shrink
  into a fixed, signed (Dale's law), biologically-sourced sparse recurrence and show the wiring
  identity, not just its density or degree sequence, matters.
* "Multiple connected brains": we scale capacity by cloning the whole fly brain K times and
  coupling clones through the real cross-hemisphere (commissural) edges only.

## Controls (fixed: neuron count, region labels, I/O pathways, edge count)
connectome (signed)  |  connectome without signs  |  degree-preserving shuffle  |  Erdos-Renyi random
|  dense  |  parameter-matched GRU.

## Experiments
E1 Blind-horizon latent prediction (8 visible steps, then 1..8 blind steps) on NAV and ARRANGE.
E2 Teacher-free planning: student-only latent MPC in the live arena, success rate.
E3 Emergent head-direction code: linear probe from EPG / CX / VIS / MB populations, visible and blind
   (path-integration) conditions.
E4 Multi-brain scaling K = 1,2,3.
E5 Ablations: sign (Dale), region input routing, horizon.

## Honest risk register (what reviewers will attack)
* Synthetic arena, not real video. Mitigation in paper: add an ego-video benchmark (e.g. Habitat / RECON)
  before submission; the pipeline is video-agnostic.
* Single teacher size (ViT-B at 256px for compute). Mitigation: ViT-L run on a GPU.
* Scale of controls: we match edges, not capacity of arbitrary architectures; GRU baseline helps.

## Status 2026-10-29 (first full pipeline run, M1 16 GB, ViT-B teacher, 20 epochs)
Full tables: results/summary.md (generated by scripts/summarize.py). Resume script: scripts/run_resume.sh.
* E1: connectome beats degree-shuffled and ER-random at every horizon, reproducible across 2 seeds
  (mean blind MSE 0.625 vs 0.678 / 0.648; sd ~0.004). Dense is worst (0.719). BUT the parameter-matched
  GRU is better (0.544), and the unsigned connectome is slightly better than the signed one (0.612 vs 0.625):
  the "wiring identity matters" claim holds; the Dale's-law claim and "better than any control" claim do not.
* E3: whole-population heading decodes at ~9.5 deg median error in the blind window for the connectome
  (shuffled 10-11, random 12-13, dense 36, GRU 37). The 51 EPG neurons alone carry almost no heading
  signal (R2 < 0.2), so no emergent ring attractor yet.
* E2: latent MPC fails for every model (nav 3-7 %, at or below random 7 %; arrange 0 %). Scripted oracle
  100 % / 93 %. The planner (goal latent from student, CEM over 6 steps) needs rework before E2 is meaningful.
* E4: K=2 gives no gain over K=1 (0.621 vs 0.629). K=3 killed: 20.8k-neuron dense state matrix swaps the
  16 GB machine (ep 9 after ~1 h); needs a sparse-matmul step or a GPU.
* Not yet integrated: wm2cx/nyc.py (NYC Midtown digital-twin arena) has no collect/eval path.
